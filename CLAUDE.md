# Sayed Jehad World (Porto 2)

Sayed's personal portfolio: an ice-themed, side-scrolling interactive resume inspired by Robby Leonardi's interactive resume. Personal project, not a Lumofy product, so Lumofy brand rules do not apply here; the palette comes from Sayed's own scene art.

## Stack and commands
- Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack) · React 19 · TypeScript 5.9 · Tailwind 4 · GSAP 3.15 + ScrollTrigger + @gsap/react · Lenis 1.3 · Phosphor icons.
- `npm run dev` (port 3000, also in `.claude/launch.json` as "dev") · `npm run typecheck` · `npm run build`.
- Stop the dev server before `npm run build` (they share `.next`).
- Deploy target: Vercel (not set up yet; no remote, not a git repo as of 30 Sep 2026). `.vercelignore` keeps Robby's site and `public/_ref` out of an upload; set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` if the domain is not the Vercel production one (metadata, robots, sitemap use `src/lib/site.ts`).

## How it works (Robby Leonardi model, rebuilt 30 Sep 2026 at Sayed's request)
- `src/components/Experience.tsx` picks the mode: **world** (horizontal side-scroller, landscape ≥768px wide and ≥560px tall) or **story** (vertical, phones / portrait tablets / reduced motion). Rotating between them keeps your level (`worldStore.holdForSwitch`).
- No loading screen and no drop-in intro (Sayed asked for this on 30 Sep 2026): the page opens straight into the world with the avatar standing and a wooden "Scroll your mouse wheel to walk" sign (`ui/WheelHint.tsx`) that fades once you set off.
- Movement mirrors rleonardi.com/interactive-resume (his main.js: horizontal phase → corner turn into a vertical balloon ascent → walk-off to the contact cloud): scroll offset `s` maps 1:1 onto a camera path (`camAt` in spec.ts) with three phases: **walk** (camera right), **ride** (a red cable car climbs the diagonal cable up the Ice Mountain, camera follows on x and y), **arrive** (step off at the top station, walk behind the sign to the peak). The player stays at a fixed screen x (46% of width), walks/skates/runs/jumps from how the world moves, turns when you scroll up. Smoothed by Lenis (sideways trackpad swipes count too). No holds.
- Other inputs: hold ← → / A D to walk at a steady pace, ↑ / W / Space or a click on the avatar to jump, sideways touch swipes in world mode.
- `src/components/world/spec.ts` builds the whole world for the viewport: Sayed's 5 scene images as set pieces joined by corridors (forest with an ABOUT ME title and a rock ledge → Bab Al Bahrain gate → Manama → promenade → **frozen Gulf** with a ROAD TO LUMOFY title, where he skates past education + every earlier role over a year timeline in the ice → gym (proof captions on the painted posters) → hall with a SKILLS neon, plyo boxes and a punching bag → office lobby/lab → yard + valley station → ride → summit), walkable `solids` (ramps across doors between floors of different heights), prop placements, `titles`, `critters`, content `anchors`, speech `bubbles`, the `ride` geometry, HUD `levelStarts`/`levelJumps`. Sizes are in units of viewport height `u`; the cable car/stations use character units (`CABIN`).
- `src/components/world/physics.ts`: time-based gravity/jumps on two feet (a small span either side of his centre). Hops onto higher ground crouch first when the scroll will carry him onto the step, otherwise he walks up and hops when his front foot reaches the edge (so notch-by-notch scrolling never hops in place). Disabled during the ride (feet = cabin floor).
- `src/components/world/WorldStage.tsx`: sticky stage + per-frame ticker (2D camera, parallax `[data-parallax]` layers, physics + teleport snap after camera cuts, character state, cabin position + sway + sliding doors, pop-in triggers, bubble placement that dodges boards, level toast, footprints, breath/spray particles, reactive critters, the AI Lab drone, the Gulf timeline fill, altitude sky, summit flag + firework shells, HUD, optional sound). `window.__sjw = { W, top0 }` is exposed in dev and in production with `?qa` in the URL, for the QA scripts.
- `src/components/world/sections.tsx`: resume content as in-world boards. `[data-pop]` elements (rise / drop / pop / slide / swing) fade in (opacity only, so their links stay in the Tab order) when their `data-x` reaches 92% of the screen and reverse once fully behind you (replaying from either side, Robby's rule); `[data-item]` children stagger in right behind. Tabbing to a link travels the world there and pins its board. Boards grow with tall screens (`Z`, up to 1.6x). Also the section titles, the gym poster captions, company-coloured Gulf stops, skills as inventory slots, whiteboard notes, stack tiles, certificate pennants on the ride and the summit card (with a copy-email button).
- `src/components/world/scenery.tsx` + `assets.ts` + `props.generated.ts`: parallax backdrop, mountainside + pylons + cable + cloud bank, corridor walls/floors from seamless tiles (feathered into the painted scenes), props (SVGs in `public/props`), painted-scene extras (spinning WTC turbines, gym fan), server LEDs, the hologram screen, doors, pillars, the two-layer `Cabin` and the summit sign cut-out. `critters.tsx`: flamingos, bulbuls, hare, camel, fish under the ice, punching bag (art in `public/props/life`).
- Feel details: Lenis lerp is set per zone every frame (0.14 on land, 0.06 on the Gulf ice, 0.1 on the lift); walk cycles are locked to distance up to ~1100 px/s and switch to a run stride above 700 px/s; the zone gesture (wave, point, dumbbells) waits 1.6 s of stillness; camera spring bump on the Gulf entrance and heavy landings; platforms squash when he lands; board tops are kept below the HUD (`safe()`). Heavy layers are `memo`ised and in-world glass has no backdrop-filter (`[data-world]`).
- HUD (`Hud.tsx`): level trail with checkpoints kept ≥36px apart, "Pause animations" (WCAG 2.2.2; `lib/motion.ts`, `html[data-motion=off]` stops the ambient loops and snowfall), sound toggle (`lib/sfx.ts`, off by default, silent until a click or key press), Quick resume, CV.
- `src/components/character/Character.tsx`: the avatar redrawn as a layered SVG from Sayed's character sheet; poses are procedural (idle, walk, run, skate, jump, wave, lift, point, flag, cheer), profile view when moving; walk/skate cycles are phase-locked to distance. `/lab` (dev only) shows every pose.
- `src/components/world/scenes.ts` is only used by the mobile story mode now.
- Content lives only in `src/data/resume.ts`, taken from the CV (wording kept verbatim, incl. serial commas). No invented numbers.
